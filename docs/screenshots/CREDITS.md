# Screenshot credits

The screenshots were taken from a local demo server, not from a real library. They only use
public-domain works and public-domain cover images.

- **Books:** all titles shown are public-domain works (authors who died long ago, editions
  published before 1903). Chapter titles come from the original texts. Narrator names, durations
  and listening progress are made up for the demo.
- **Cover images:** historical covers, title pages and illustrations marked as public domain on
  Wikimedia Commons:
